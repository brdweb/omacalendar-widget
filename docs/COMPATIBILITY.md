# Compatibility

| Widget version | OmaCalendar app version | IPC major | Status |
|---|---|---:|---|
| `0.1.0` | `1.0.0` | 2 | Stable |

Requires Omarchy 4.0.0 or newer, Quickshell 0.3.1 or newer and the native
OmaCalendar service with IPC 2.0. The app window does not need to stay open.
App and widget versions are independent; incompatible IPC majors are rejected.
